How it works

Talk first, then find help

Solace listens before it advises, and only brings up schemes when you want them.

1

Talk it out

Share how you're feeling. Solace listens first and helps you reflect, without judging.

2

Get practical help

Name what's weighing on you, like money, childcare, housing or work, and Solace offers options that fit.

3

Find support

When you need a person, Solace points you to services in Singapore that can help.

Resources

Support you can use

Some schemes and services that can help. Solace can also point you to the ones that fit your situation.

Childcare and family support

Infant and Childcare Subsidies

Tiered subsidies for infant care and childcare fees at ECDA-licensed centres. Working single parents can get an Additional Subsidy based on household income.

Visit ECDA →

Check your childcare subsidy

See which childcare and infant care subsidies you may qualify for and how to apply.

Visit SupportGoWhere →

Family Service Centres and ComCare

Call, walk in or email an FSC for family and social support. They can also link you to ComCare and KidSTART. ComCare hotline: 1800 222 0000.

Visit MSF →

Support for single unwed parents

Children of single unwed parents get Child Development Account benefits, including the $3,000 First Step Grant, and unwed mothers get 16 weeks of maternity leave.

Visit MSF →

Work and leave

Childcare Leave

Up to 6 days of paid childcare leave a year for a child under 7, and 2 days of extended childcare leave for a child aged 7 to 12.

Visit MOM →

Parental leave for single parents

Single unwed mothers can take the full 10 weeks of shared parental leave or its cash equivalent. Divorced parents qualify for all Government-Paid leave schemes.

Visit MSF →

Maternity Leave

16 weeks of Government-Paid Maternity Leave if your child is a Singapore citizen and you meet the work requirements.

Visit MOM →

Flexible work requests

Since December 2024, employers must have a process for flexible work requests and consider them fairly, such as working from home or staggered hours.

Read the guide →

Housing

HDB Public Rental Scheme

Heavily subsidised rental flats for single parents aged 21 and above with no family support or other housing options.

Visit HDB →

Proximity Housing Grant

Up to $15,000 in CPF grants to buy a resale flat near or with your parents, so family can help out.

Visit HDB →

Housing grants for singles

The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant gives up to $40,000 to help buy a new or resale flat.

Visit CPF →

Other housing schemes for single parents

An overview of schemes like the Parenthood Provisional Housing Scheme (temporary flats while waiting for a BTO) and ASSIST (BTO quota for divorced or widowed parents).
